# Welcome to  Devoric! 👋

We're so glad you're here. This page is your starting point for getting settled in, finding your way around, and making the most of your time with us.

## First Things First

- **Get your accounts set up** — Make sure you have access to your email, chat, and any tools your mentor shared with you.
- **Meet your mentor** — You'll have someone guiding you day to day. Don't be shy about asking them anything.
- **Join the team channels** — Say hi! A quick intro goes a long way.
- **Read the onboarding docs** — Your team will point you to the right places.

## How We Work

- **Ask questions.** Seriously — there are no dumb ones. Everyone here was new once.
- **Take your time.** You're here to learn, not to know everything on day one.
- **Break things in dev, not prod.** Experiment freely, just be mindful in shared environments.
- **Communicate early.** Stuck on something? Say so. It's a strength, not a weakness.
- **Ship small, learn fast.** Little wins add up.

## Tips for a Great Internship

- 🗓️ **Show up curious** — engage in meetings, even if you're just listening.
- 📝 **Keep notes** — future you will thank present you.
- 🔁 **Ask for feedback** — early and often. It's how you grow fastest.
- 🧠 **Follow your interests** — if something excites you, dig in.
- ☕ **Take breaks** — rest is part of doing good work.
- 🤝 **Build relationships** — your teammates are your best resource.


## You Belong Here

You were chosen because we believe in what you bring to the table. Come as you are, bring your ideas, and don't be afraid to make your mark. We're excited to see what you'll do.

Welcome aboard. 🎉
